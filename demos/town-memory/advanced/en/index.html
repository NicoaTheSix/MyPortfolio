From roof ridge to workbench

From the temple courtyard, you can still make out the glazed flowers and birds on the roof. In the workshop, you can see the clay, tools and glazes up close.

Record where each piece sits, who made it and what neighbors call it.

The old hillside trail

Behind the village, stone steps lead uphill through bamboo. A fork may once have led to fields, a neighboring village or a shortcut children took home from school.

Record old trail names, forks and how the path looked before and after repairs.

By the creek after dark

Once it gets dark, a light sometimes flashes in the grass. When you return, note the date, weather, vegetation and nearby lighting.

Stay on the trail and keep bright lights away from the grass.

The Koji ceramics festival

Workshops bring their pieces into the village courtyard. Adults watch a potter trim the clay while children try shaping a piece of their own.

Alongside photos, keep the day's program and labels from the exhibits.
