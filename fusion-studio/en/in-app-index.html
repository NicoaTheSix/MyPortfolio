Workspace tabs and sidebar visibility#

ATLAS case studies now use a peer workspace tab. Settings → Sidebar content independently toggles ATT&CK, ATLAS and CAR, with all three enabled by default; Workplace always remains. Collapsed selection groups keep the summary and controls compact. Guide.

CAR Browser opens beside Technique Details as an independent upper reference tab. Close it with × or its context menu; Workplace reopens it. Recovery retains tab visibility, active page and category. See the workspace guide.
